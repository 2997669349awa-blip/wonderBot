## Welcome to WonderBot

Thanks for using WonderBot!

WonderBot is a QQ-group assistant with built-in **cost auditing**, **long-term memory**
and a **sender allowlist**.

WonderBot is an independent open-source derivative released under **AGPL-3.0-or-later**.
Source code, releases and update notices are served from our own repository:
[**2997669349awa-blip/wonderBot**](https://github.com/2997669349awa-blip/wonderBot).

It is maintained by the WonderBot team and is not affiliated with any upstream project
or its official team. Any paid deployment, customization or support service will be
disclosed explicitly on our service page.

Privacy: WonderBot does not upload usage statistics to any external service by default.
Cost: token usage and cost of every LLM call are recorded in a local ledger.
